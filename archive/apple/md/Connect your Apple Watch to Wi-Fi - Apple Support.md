# Connect your Apple Watch to Wi-Fi

When your iPhone isn't nearby, your Apple Watch can connect to Wi-Fi. To
choose which Wi-Fi network your Apple Watch connects to, you need
watchOS 5 or later.

## Choose a Wi-Fi network

1.  Open the Settings app on your Apple Watch.

2.  Tap Wi-Fi. Your device automatically searches for networks.

    <img
    src="https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/7WUAS350/images/apple-watch/watchos-9-2-series-8-settings-wifi.png"
    class="gb-image" style="max-width: min(100%,225px);" width="450"
    height="502" alt="Apple Watch Wi-Fi settings screen" />

3.  Tap the name of the network that you want to join. If you have Apple
    Watch Series 6 or later, you can connect to 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi
    networks. Apple Watch Series 5 and earlier, and Apple Watch SE, can
    connect only to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi networks.

4.  If asked, enter the password using
    <a href="https://support.apple.com/kb/HT206907"
    class="gb-anchor">Scribble</a> or the Apple Watch keyboard.

5.  Tap Join.

<div class="note gb-note">

You can use Scribble in these languages: English (Australia, Canada, the
United Kingdom, and the United States), French (France and Canada),
German, and Italian.

</div>

## Forget a Wi-Fi network

1.  Open the Settings app on your Apple Watch.

2.  Tap Wi-Fi, then tap the name of the network you’re connected to.

3.  Tap Forget This Network, then tap Forget.

    <img
    src="https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/7WUAS350/images/apple-watch/watchos-9-2-series-8-settings-wifi-forget-this-network.png"
    class="gb-image" style="max-width: min(100%,225px);" width="450"
    height="502" alt="Option to Forget This Network on Apple Watch" />

## Find compatible Wi-Fi networks

Your Apple Watch can connect to a Wi-Fi network:

- If your iPhone, while connected to your watch with Bluetooth, has
  connected to the network before.

- If the Wi-Fi network is 802.11b/g/n 2.4GHz (or 5GHz if you have Apple
  Watch Series 6 or later).

Your Apple Watch won't connect to public networks that require logins,
subscriptions, or profiles. These networks, called captive networks, can
include free and pay networks in places like businesses, schools, dorms,
apartments, hotels, and stores. When your Apple Watch connects to a
compatible Wi-Fi network instead of your iPhone connection, the
<span class="inline-icon gb-inline-icon">Wi-Fi icon<img
src="https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/7WUAS350/images/apple-watch/watchos4-wifi-status-bar-icon.png"
class="gb-inline-icon-image" style="max-width: 24px" width="60"
height="48" alt="the Wi-Fi icon" /></span> appears in the
<a href="https://support.apple.com/kb/HT206951"
class="gb-anchor">Control Center</a>.

## Adjust auto hotspot settings

1.  Open the Settings app on your Apple Watch.

2.  Scroll down and tap Wi-Fi.

3.  Scroll to Auto Hotspot Settings, then tap to choose an option.

    <img
    src="https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/7WUAS350/images/apple-watch/watchos-9-2-series-8-settings-wifi-auto-hotspot-settings.png"
