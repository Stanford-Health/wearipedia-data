### Set up a Fitbit device on a new account

<div class="note">

<div class="no-margin">

**Note**: When you connect certain Fitbit devices to an Android phone, a
notification on your phone appears to begin the setup process. Tap the
notification and follow the on-screen instructions to set up your
device. If you don't have the Fitbit app, the notification takes you to
the Google Play Store to download the app.

</div>

</div>

1.  Connect your Fitbit device to the charger before you begin the setup
    process.
2.  Download and install the Fitbit app from one of the locations below:
    - Apple devices: <a

    - Android devices: <a
      href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fitbit.FitbitMobile"
      rel="noopener" target="_blank">Google Play Store</a>
3.  Open the Fitbit app and tap **Sign in with Google**.
4.  Choose a Google Account. If you don't have a Google Account, tap
    **Sign in** <img
    src="//lh3.googleusercontent.com/ugLkII9LIMUgvXFf-IXgYyZ4PJQZEWtLe8F6_SaW1VKFWzvkj9K-qbgB0_ZzwqrM4h8=h36"

    height="18" /> **Create account.**
5.  Follow the on-screen instructions to create an account and connect
    ("pair") your Fitbit device to your phone. This ensures your
    Fitbit device and phone can communicate with one another (sync their

weight, and sex to calculate your stride length and to estimate

<span id="exp" class="zippy">Using multiple Fitbit devices on one
account</span>

<div>

<div>

<div class="alert">

<div class="no-margin">

**Important:** You can't connect multiple devices that use the Fitbit
Gallery to one account. For Fitbit Charge series, Fitbit Inspire series,
Fitbit Luxe, Fitbit Sense series, Fitbit Versa series, and Google Pixel
Watch series, only connect one of these devices to your account at a
time.

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div>

If you connect more than one Fitbit device to the same account, note the
following:

- If you forget to remove one Fitbit device before you put on another,
  the overlap of data is reconciled to provide the most accurate
  information possible. For best results, don't wear more than one
  device at a time and sync each device before you remove it and wear
  another.
- Only one person can use a Fitbit account. When you create an account,
  you provide personal information, such as height and weight, to
  calculate certain data.
- If you have more than one Fitbit device that receives notifications
  from your phone, notifications might appear on one or more of your
  Fitbit devices.
- A Fitbit device can only pair with one phone. If you have multiple
  phones, you receive notifications only from the phone connected to
  your Fitbit device.

<span id="aria-sync" class="zippy">Aria</span>

#### What router settings do I need to set up and sync Aria?

Aria connects to your wireless home network and works with any Wi-Fi
router that uses the following standards:

- 802.11b frequency setting
- WEP / WPA / WPA2 personal security
- Automatic (DHCP) IP setup (static IP configurations aren't supported)

Your SSID and password should contain only alphanumeric characters (e.g.
A-Z, 0-9). If you experience issues with a network that uses special
characters, change the name of the network to only use alphanumeric
characters, and try to connect again.

#### Can I use a mobile Wi-Fi hotspot to set up Aria?

A mobile Wi-Fi hotspot should only be used if a wireless router is
unavailable. Consider the following information when you set up your
scale through a hotspot:

- Connecting Aria to a mobile Wi-Fi hotspot requires that the mobile
  device be persistently in this mode. The device’s battery may drain
  quickly.
- To complete the setup process with a mobile Wi-Fi hotspot, you need a
  second wireless device to connect to the hotspot. For example, if you
  use an iPhone as a hotspot, you need either another mobile device or a
  computer to proceed through either of the set up methods explained in
  <a href="#setup-aria" rel="noopener">How do I set up Aria?</a>
- Your hotspot needs to be configured similar to the same specifications
  as a wireless router. For the specs, go to
  <a href="#aria-sync" rel="noopener">What router settings do I need to
  set up and sync Aria?</a>

### <span id="setup-aria"></span>How do I set up Aria?

<div>

To set up your scale through the web-based method, you need a Wi-Fi
enabled computer, phone, or tablet. Make sure you know your Wi-Fi
password and that it doesn't exceed 31 characters.

<div class="note">

<div class="no-margin">

**Note:** If you want to set up your Aria again after you make changes
to your network, go to
<a href="https://support.google.com/fitbit/answer/14236805"
rel="noopener">How do I use my Fitbit scale?</a>

</div>

</div>

To set up your Aria scale:

1.  Restart the computer, phone, or tablet that you want to use to set
    up your scale.
2.  Make sure your browser allows cookies.
3.  If you use a computer:
    1.  Place your scale within 10 feet of your router.
    2.  Ensure that your router is compatible with the 802.11b protocol.
        For more information, go to
        <a href="#aria-sync" rel="noopener">What router settings do I need to
        set up and sync Aria?</a>
    3.  If the router is connected to your computer with an Ethernet
        cable, unplug it. The router and computer must be connected over
        Wi-Fi.
4.  Open a web browser on your computer, phone, or tablet and go to
    <a href="http://www.fitbit.com/scale/setup/start" rel="noopener"
    target="_blank">Fitbit scale setup</a>.
5.  Tap **Get started**, then tap it again when you find the pop-up
    message.
6.  Sign in to your fitbit.com account.
7.  Personalize your Aria with a name and the initials you'd like the
    scale to display when it recognizes you.
8.  Remove a battery from your Aria scale and reinsert it after 10
    seconds to put the scale into setup mode. Tap **Continue** when the
    scale's display says "SETUP ACTIVE."
9.  Switch your computer, phone, or tablet to the scale's Wi-Fi network
    (this will appear as the word "Aria" followed by 6 characters).
10. Return to your web browser and tap **I'm Connected**. You'll find a
    list of available wireless networks.
11. Tap the name of your home wireless network. If you can't find your
    home wireless network, tap **Refresh Networks** to scan again. If
    you still can't find your network, tap **Join Other Network** to
    enter your network name and password manually.
12. Enter your network password. Remember that it's case-sensitive.
13. Tap **Submit**, and then immediately check your scale's screen as it
    attempts to connect to your network.
    - If you find a check mark or a smiley face, Aria is connected and
      you can stand on the scale to complete a weigh-in.

#### Router settings

Aria 2 connects to your wireless home network and works with any Wi-Fi
router that uses the following standards:

- 802.11b/g/n frequency setting
- WEP / WPA / WPA2 personal security
- Automatic (DHCP) IP setup (static IP configurations aren't supported)
- Both the network name and password must be no more than 32 characters
  long

We don't recommend a mobile Wi-Fi hotspot to set up Aria 2, as it may
slow down the sync process and prevent an update. The use of a mobile
Wi-Fi hotspot requires the device to remain persistently in this mode,
which may drain the battery quickly and use excessive data.
