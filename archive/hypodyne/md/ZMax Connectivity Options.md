## ZMax Connectivity Options

<div class="pdiv">

## Use case: researcher and patient at the same location

When the researcher and patient are at the same location, internet
connectivity is not necessary. <sup>Z</sup>Max can either stream data
wirelessly to the researcher's PC or store data internally to the memory
card. In both cases, signal quality can first be previewed wirelessly.

<img src="connectivity1.png" width="393" />
<img src="dongle.jpg" class="bw" style="margin-left:40px" />

  

## Use case: remote researcher dials patient's device

In this case the researcher and patient are at different locations but
both have internet connectivity. The researcher is the caller and the
patient the receiver. The patient first starts the <sup>Z</sup>Max
server on a Windows PC or Android mobile device. Then communicates
connection information to the researcher. The researcher dials the
patient's IP address and a connection is established. Data begins
streaming automatically. The patient can go to sleep. As in the previous
examples, the researcher can decide to continue monitoring the data as
it is being saved on his or her hard disk, or to initiate internal
memory card storage, in which case the monitoring and disk storage
terminate and the researcher can leave, confident that the data will be
recorded without requiring further intervention.

<img src="connectivity2.png" style="margin-bottom:20px" width="593" />  
<img src="recordercall.png" class="bw" />

  

## Use case: patient dials researcher's PC

As in the previous case, the researcher and patient are at different
locations but both have internet connectivity. The patient is the caller
and the researcher the receiver. The researcher first starts the
<sup>Z</sup>Max recording software on a Windows PC. Then communicates
connection information to the patient. The patient dials the
researcher's IP address and a connection is established. Data begins
streaming automatically. The patient can go to sleep. As in the previous
examples, the researcher can decide to continue monitoring the data as
it is being saved on his or her hard disk, or to initiate internal
memory card storage, in which case the monitoring and disk storage
terminate and the researcher can leave, confident that the data will be
recorded without requiring further intervention.  
  
The advantage when the patient is the caller is that connectivity
restrictions on the patient's router, firewall and at the ISP level are
bypassed. Additionally, because the <sup>Z</sup>Max server is also an
Android application, Mobile Data can be used to stream the data (about
200MB for a night's recording). This simplifies the process because the
researcher never has to diagnose connectivity issues on the patient's
side. The researcher can even provide a pre-configured mobile device to
the user if needed.
