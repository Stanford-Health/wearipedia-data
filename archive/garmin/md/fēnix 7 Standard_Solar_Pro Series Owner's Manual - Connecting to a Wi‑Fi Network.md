# Connecting to a Wi‑Fi Network

<div class="body taskbody">

<div id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__PREREQ_64527E3907B04726BC0278FA27C0A952"
class="section prereq p">

You must connect your watch to the Garmin Connect™ app on your phone or
to the Garmin Express™ application on your computer before you can
connect to a Wi‑Fi<sup>®</sup> network.

</div>

1.  <span id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__STEP_8818AB3A425F4EF3982F8099B5C76215"><span class="ph cmd"><span class="ph">Hold
    <span class="ph uicontrol">MENU</span>.</span> </span></span>
2.  <span id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__STEP_89F84D80164D410C8710586FC3FA3B20"><span class="ph cmd">Select
    <span class="ph menucascade"><span class="ph uicontrol">Connectivity</span><span class="abbr"
    title="and then"> \>
    </span><span class="ph uicontrol">Wi-Fi</span><span class="abbr"
    title="and then"> \> </span><span class="ph uicontrol">My
    Networks</span><span class="abbr" title="and then"> \>
    </span><span class="ph uicontrol">Add Network</span></span>.
    </span></span>
    <div class="itemgroup stepresult">

    The watch displays a list of nearby Wi‑Fi networks.

    </div>
3.  <span id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__STEP_37086F8BCF6F4002BADC72A20F902724"><span class="ph cmd">Select
    a network. </span></span>
4.  <span id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__STEP_544B8B68D43044DA8431536EDFE43A21"><span class="ph cmd">If
    necessary, enter the password for the network. </span></span>

<div id="GUID-A320BB2B-B192-4CDF-931E-52FC01144D1E__RESULT_56A703D4D3634C4D870B9A1C37948F35"
class="section section result">

The watch connects to the network, and the network is added to the list
of saved networks. The watch reconnects to this network automatically
when it is within range.

</div>

</div>
