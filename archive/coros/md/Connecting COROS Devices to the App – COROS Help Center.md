# Connecting COROS Devices to the App

<div class="article-author">

<div class="article-meta">

- March 19, 2025 13:06
- Updated

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="section article-info">

<div class="article-content">

<div class="article-body">

## COROS App Compatibility Requirements

A smartphone running on the Apple iOS (minimum version iOS 13) or
Android operating system (Android 5.0 or above) and the COROS app is
required to set up and use your COROS watch. It is not possible to sync
your workout data from the watch with a USB.

**Note:** Using the COROS App on a device with an unsupported operating
system may lead to reduced functionality. In addition, if you remove the
app while your device is running an unsupported OS version, you might
not be able to reinstall it from the app store.

> ***COROS Support Insights***
>
> Coming in 2025, the minimum Android operating system requirement will
> update to version 6.0 and above. COROS users that cannot update the
> operating system on their smart device can continue using their
> current app version. 

## Getting Started

Please follow the steps below to connect the COROS device with your
smartphone.

<div class="section supports-btmline">

1.  1.  Make sure your phone is connected to Wi-Fi or a cellular
        network.
    2.  Turn on the Bluetooth function on your phone.
    3.  Download and install the COROS app
        from <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coros/id1277625343"
        target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">iOS App Store</a> or <a
        href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yf.smart.coros.dist&amp;hl=en_US"
        target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Play Store</a>.
        - Android users can download the apk file directly via the link
          below to install the COROS app. 
        - <a
          href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AuMRp92HEf-hmHCdeiMZ3ZtcC7P4IW7i/view"
          target="_blank"
          rel="noopener noreferrer">https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AuMRp92HEf-hmHCdeiMZ3ZtcC7P4IW7i/view</a>
    4.  Open the COROS app and log into your COROS account.
    5.  Navigate to the Profile Page (icon in the bottom lefthand corner
        of the screen), and tap on "Add New Device."
    6.  Use the COROS app to scan the QR code on your COROS device.
        - If you no longer have the QR code page on your device, here’s
          how to bring it back on – main device face \> System \> Pair
          Phone.
        - If you don’t wish to scan the QR code, you can also tap on
          “Pair COROS Device Manually” and select the correct device on
          the list.
    7.  Accept the Bluetooth pairing request on your phone and grant
        necessary access to your COROS device.
        - Without this selected, certain features including
          notifications and pairing may not be functional during usage. 
    8.  Complete the system setup
        - Including: wearing hand, digital dial location, notifications
          preference and others on the COROS app.
    9.  Update the firmware on your COROS device (if needed).

</div>

<div class="section supports-btmline">

## Disconnect Your COROS device

Please follow the steps below to disconnect the COROS device from your
smartphone:

- Open the COROS app and go to the Profile Page.
- Tap on the device image and scroll down to select "Remove."
  - Removing your device from the COROS app will not delete the data
    saved to your COROS account.
- Open Bluetooth settings page on your phone and forget/delete your
  COROS device.

</div>

<div class="section supports-btmline">

## Pair Your COROS device with a New Phone or COROS Account

If you have updated your phone or smart device, please follow the
instructions below to successfully transfer your COROS device: 

- Disconnect your COROS device from the old phone or COROS account
  properly.
  - <a
    href="https://support.coros.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046709991-How-to-remove-your-COROS-watch-properly-from-paired-smart-device"
    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">How to remove your COROS
    device properly from paired smart device</a>
- Log into your COROS account on the new phone.
- Follow the pairing process listed above.

</div>

## Troubleshooting Guide and FAQ

<div class="accordion_block">

<div class="accordion_title">

I received the error message: "Cannot connect to the server. Please try
later."

</div>

<div class="accordion_content">

Please follow the steps listed below:

- Disable any connected VPN.
- Switch the network on your phone (cellular/WiFi).
- Please change the settings on your iPhone.
  - Settings \> Safari \> Clear History and Website Data.

</div>

</div>

<div class="accordion_block">

<div class="accordion_title">

I am experiencing Bluetooth and notification issues

</div>

<div class="accordion_content">

Please follow the steps below to reconfigure the Bluetooth connection:

- On the Device page from the COROS app, tap on the device image and
  locate the “Remove” option down below. Tap on it to remove the device
  from the COROS app.
- On your smartphone’s Bluetooth setting page, forget/delete the device.
- Restart both the phone and the device.
- Follow the pairing process listed above to connect the device.

Alternatively, you can also try performing a hard restart on your
device. Press and hold the Power button for at least 15 seconds, or
until you see the COROS logo.

</div>

</div>

<div class="accordion_block">

<div class="accordion_title">

Concerns Regarding iPhone

</div>

<div class="accordion_content">

Please go to the system settings on your iPhone and check if you have
disabled Bluetooth access for the COROS app.

- On your iPhone \> Settings \> Privacy \> Bluetooth \> COROS \> ON.
- On your iPhone \> Settings \> Bluetooth \> COROS device \> "i" \>
  Share System Notifications \> ON.

</div>

</div>

<div class="accordion_block">

<div class="accordion_title">

Concerns Regarding Android

</div>

<div class="accordion_content">

Unfortunately, there is a known occurrence with Android 10 and 11 users
on Bluetooth connection issues, including failure to reconnect after
disconnection. COROS has made no changes to the COROS app and products
in terms of Bluetooth connectivity. After testing and investigation, we
have concluded that the Bluetooth connection issue was caused by the
Android 10 and 11 Bluetooth compatibility affecting all device types
with BLE connection.

<span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;">It
is unfortunate that as the manufacturer of Bluetooth devices, COROS has
limited solutions to fix this unless Android releases new updates to
Android 10 and 11 users to improve the Bluetooth compatibility. Android
is known to release major updates without fully testing the
compatibility with all devices. Good news is that Android may release a
new update soon.</span>

Although this isn’t an issue with the device itself, here are some
solutions to help in the meantime. Please make sure to update the
Android system version once new updates are available. The COROS
engineering team is also in contact with the Android team for this
matter. You can also try the following steps to see if they fix the
issue temporarily. It’s not guaranteed but this is the best we can do
for now.

- Remove the device on the COROS app.
- Restart the phone.
- Enter airplane mode on your phone.
- Enable GPS/Location services.
- Wait for 30 seconds and exit airplane mode.
- Pair the device on the COROS app via “Pair COROS Device Manually”
  instead of scanning the QR code.

</div>

</div>

<div class="accordion_block">

<div class="accordion_title">

Bluetooth Headset Compatibility

</div>

<div class="accordion_content">

Bluetooth headsets use Bluetooth 2.0 (Classic Bluetooth) and smart
wearables generally use Bluetooth 4.0/5.0 (BLE). These two protocols and
systems are different.

</div>

</div>
