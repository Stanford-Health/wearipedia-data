# Sleep (U.S.) - Does my device emit Bluetooth or Wi-Fi signals while I sleep?

</div>

<div class="section article-info">

<div class="article-content">

<div id="article-body" class="article-body">

When it is first plugged into power, Sleep will be discoverable via
Bluetooth for 30 minutes so that it can be set up. After the setup, data
is transmitted via Wi-Fi and the device is no longer discoverable via
Bluetooth.  
  
Sleep does not maintain a continuous Wi-Fi connection with your router.
A Wi-Fi connection is used during the setup, and to transfer data as
needed. Sleep doesn't emit Wi-Fi signals while you sleep. However, it
emits short-burst Wi-Fi signals to trigger IFTTT events when you get in
and out of the bed.
