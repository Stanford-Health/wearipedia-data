# ScanWatch - Installing my device

</div>

<div class="section article-info">

<div class="article-content">

<div id="article-body" class="article-body">

**Disclaimer**: Due to health regulations and clearances, some ScanWatch
features may not be available or may not have clinical validation in
your region.
<a href="https://support.withings.com/hc/articles/360013982637"
target="_self">Learn more</a>.

  
New owner of a ScanWatch? Congrats! And welcome to a healthier -and
happier- life.

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Now, to install this brand new watch,
please perform the following steps:</span>

1.  <span style="font-weight: 400;">Open the Withings App and tap
    </span>**Sign up** <span style="font-weight: 400;">if you are new to
    Withings. Follow the instructions until your land on the **Home**
    screen.</span>
2.  <span style="font-weight: 400;">Your device can be detected
    automatically. If so, tap **Install** and go to step 7.</span>
3.  If not, tap the **Devices** icon in the top right corner of the
    screen.  
    ![DevicesIcon.png](/hc/article_attachments/27922930005521)
4.  <span style="font-weight: 400;">Tap </span>**+** at the top
    right-hand corner of the screen<span style="font-weight: 400;">.  
    </span>
5.  Tap **Watches**.
6.  Tap **ScanWatch**.
7.  Tap **Install**.
8.  Your ScanWatch is detected and needs to be connected to your mobile
    device. Tap **Pair**.
9.  Enter the code displayed on the screen of your watch. Again, tap
    **Pair**.
10. Please wait during the pairing process.
11. Look at the hands of your ScanWatch to see if they are properly
    positioned. Minute and hour hands should be positioned at noon,
    while the activity dial one should be positioned to 100%. Tap
    **Yes** or **No**.
12. If you select **No**, set all the hands to noon or 100% by twisting
    the dial.
13. Now ScanWatch displays the correct time.
14. Please wait during the device configuration.
15. Keep in mind that it can take a minute or two. Also, make sure to
    keep the app open and in the foreground during this process.
16. Choose on which arm you would like to wear your ScanWatch.
17. If you don't wear it just yet, try it on and make sure to wear it
    <a href="https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017770597"
    target="_self">properly</a>.
18. You're all set! Get started with the
    <a href="https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017027738"
    target="_self">tutorials</a> now or later, as you wish.
19. You can access the features again in **Devices \> ScanWatch \>
    Tutorials**.  
    Note that you won't be able to use the features before activating
    them via Tutorials.
