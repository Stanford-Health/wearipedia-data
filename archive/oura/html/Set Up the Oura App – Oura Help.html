Set Up the Oura App

The Oura App is supported on iOS devices running Apple iOS 15 and higher, or Android devices running Android 9 and higher with Google Play services. Your device must support Bluetooth 4.0.

Set Up Your Oura Account 

  1. Download the free Oura App from the App Store (iOS) or the Google Play Store (Android)
  2. Open the Oura App and tap Start
  1. If you're new to Oura, enter an email address to use for your Oura account and tap Continue
    • This email can differ from the one used to purchase your Oura Ring and will serve as your login across all Oura services, including your membership
    • If you're already an Oura member, enter the email associated with your Oura account and follow the login prompts
    • If you purchased a prepaid membership with your ring, it will automatically be applied to the first Oura account you create during setup. Once the membership is linked to an account, it cannot be transferred to a different Oura account
  2. On the next screen, accept the terms and conditions by selecting the first toggle. If you'd like to receive Oura usage tips and special offers in your email, select the second toggle as well
  3. To set up a new Oura Ring, follow the in-app instructions or see this article for more information
  4. Once you've completed setting up your Oura Ring, tap Send setup email
  5. Check your inbox for an account set up email. In the email, select the Set up account button or the verification link to take you to a verification page
  6. Enter the billing address associated with a payment method you'll use for your Oura Membership
    • Some members are not required to enter billing or payment information (for example, members with Lifetime Membership)
  7. Enter your home address (required for sales tax calculations)
  8. Select your billing preference (monthly or annual) and then enter a payment method
    • You won't be charged until the end of your first complimentary month. If you have a prepaid membership, you won't be charged until the end of your prepaid period
  9. Review and confirm that the information you've entered is correct, then tap Submit
  10. Return to the Oura App and tap Continue. If you're using an iPhone, you'll have the option to enable facial or touch recognition for faster sign-in

Once you've returned to the Oura App, your account and membership setup are complete.

Continue on to the next screen in the Oura App to personalize your Oura experience and finish the app setup.

Personalize Your Oura Experience

  1. On the next screen, select Begin to sync your data. This process may take a few minutes
    • If the Oura App asks you to set up a new Oura Ring, follow the in-app instructions or see this article for more information
  2. You'll have the option to link your Oura App to third-party integrations, including Apple Health and Health Connect by Android. For more information, see our articles on using app integrations with Oura
  3. Enter your Date of birth, Weight, Height, and Sex assigned at birth. Tap on your desired measurement system (imperial or metric) under Units, then tap Done
    • If you decide to use a third-party app like Apple Health or Health Connect by Android, this personal information will update automatically from there. You will not be able to update this within the Oura App in the future
  4. If you are new to Oura, take a moment to answer some questions so that Oura can personalize your experience

Your Oura App is now set up.

For the best experience with Oura, set up your country of residence under Settings. Due to regulation, some features are country-specific. You can customize or change units, notifications, data sharing, and other preferences anytime in the Settings menu.
