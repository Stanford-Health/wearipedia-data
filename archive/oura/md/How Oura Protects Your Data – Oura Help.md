# How Oura Protects Your Data

<div class="article-author">

<div class="article-meta">

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="section article-info">

<div class="article-content">

<div class="article-body">

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">At Oura,
protecting your personal data is a task we take seriously. Our products
are designed to help you to track important aspects of your health like
your daily habits, reproductive health, and the quality of your sleep –
we understand that data does not get much more personal than this. We
may process personal data regarding your use of our products to protect
your privacy. This may involve the use of privacy-enhancing technologies
and other privacy-protective techniques like aggregation,
de-identification, or anonymization. When information is aggregated or
anonymized, it is no longer considered personal data.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">When we
generate data stories about Oura Ring members, this personal data is
aggregated or otherwise processed to protect user privacy. For example,
we may process insights like average Sleep Score by group, where each
group typically contains thousands of users or more. If a group is too
small to preserve anonymity, we do not include the group as a
statistic.  
</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Read about our
<a
href="https://ouraring.com/blog/ouras-commitment-to-reproductive-health/"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">commitment to data privacy on
The Pulse Blog</a>.</span>

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Data Subject Requests**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You can find
more information about your data subject rights in our
<a href="https://ouraring.com/privacy-policy-oura-health"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a>. Data
subject requests may address access, deletion, and rectification of your
personal information. Please specify the scope of your data subject
request in your inquiry; this helps us respond to your request most
efficiently.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you would
like to submit a data subject request, please contact
<a href="mailto:dataprotection@ouraring.com" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">dataprotection@ouraring.com</a>.</span>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **<span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Delete Account</span>**

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You can delete
your Oura account from within the app:</span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Tap the
    menu
    <img src="/hc/article_attachments/28565670532883" width="21" height="15"
    alt="Icon Bars Menu.png" /> in the upper left corner of the Oura
    App</span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select
    **Settings**</span>
3.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Scroll
    down to and select **Delete account and all data**</span>
4.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select
    **Delete** in the confirmation box</span>
5.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select
    **Yes, delete my Oura account** in the second confirmation
    box</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You can also
delete your account through
<a href="https://cloud.ouraring.com/" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">Oura on the Web</a>:</span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Log in to
    <a href="https://cloud.ouraring.com/" target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer">Oura on the Web</a> with your account
    email</span>

2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select the
    profile
    <img src="/hc/article_attachments/23194461010323" width="20" height="20"
    alt="OOTW profile.png" /> icon in the upper-right corner</span>

3.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select
    **My Account **from the dropdown menu</span>

4.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Scroll to
    the bottom and select **Delete account**</span>

5.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Enter your
    email address in the confirmation dialogue box</span>

6.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Click **Delete**</span>

7.  Log out of Oura on the Web and the Oura App

8.  Remove the Oura App from your phone

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">***This will
permanently delete your Oura account and all account data.*** Any access
you have to <a href="/hc/en-us/articles/360025587353" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">Oura on the Web</a>,
<a href="https://get.ouraring.com/forbusiness" target="_blank"
rel="noopener">Oura Teams, Health Risk Management</a>, or
<a href="/hc/en-us/articles/4415266939155" target="_blank"
rel="noopener">the Oura API</a> will also be removed.</span>

Note:

- If you choose to cancel your Oura Membership, your data will not
  automatically be deleted. You can renew your Oura Membership at any
  time in your
  <a href="https://membership.ouraring.com/" target="_blank"
  rel="noopener noreferrer">membership settings</a>.
- If you choose to delete your Oura account, both your account and data
  will be permanently deleted, and your Oura Membership will be
  automatically canceled. 

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Oura App and Ring Data** </span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Your Oura Ring
detects certain physiological metrics, such as heart rate, average body
temperature, and movement. Your ring then syncs this data with your Oura
App via Bluetooth. All your Oura App data is connected to your Oura
account, which you created when first setting up your ring. The Oura App
periodically syncs your data with
<a href="https://cloud.ouraring.com/" target="_blank"
rel="noopener">Oura on the Web</a> to ensure your app insights are
preserved in case your ring is lost, stolen, or you decide to upgrade.
We recommend saving your data under a single Oura account so you can
switch rings and devices without ever losing your data.</span>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**More Information**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You can find
more information about how Oura protects your personal data in our
<a href="https://ouraring.com/privacy-policy-oura-health"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a>.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Please contact
<dataprotection@ouraring.com> with any questions or feedback about our
privacy practices.</span>
