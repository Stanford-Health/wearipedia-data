## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Set Up a New Ring for New Members**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you're
setting up a new ring for the first time,
m<span class="wysiwyg-color-black wysiwyg-font-size-large">ake sure you
have a stable internet connection and an Oura charger before starting.
Then follow these steps:</span></span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Download
    the Oura App** from the
    <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oura/id1043837948"
    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">iOS</a> or <a
    href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ouraring.oura&amp;hl=en_US"
    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Play App Store</a></span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Unbox**
    the ring and
    [charger](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410719638547)</span>
3.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Plug the
    charger into a USB power source**, such as a laptop or supporting
    power brick</span>

The Oura App is supported on iOS devices running Apple iOS 15 and
higher, or Android devices running Android 9 and higher with Google Play
services. Your device must support Bluetooth 4.0.

4.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Put your
    ring on the charger**. The LED will blink blue to indicate that your
    ring is ready to be paired  
    </span>
5.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black"></span>**<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Make
    sure that your device's Bluetooth is enabled  
    </span>**<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black"></span>
6.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Follow
    the** [**in-app
    instructions**](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058634153)
    to set up your Oura account</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">See this
      article on [creating an Oura
      account](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025441234)
      for more information</span>

On Android devices, you might see a prompt asking for access to your
contacts and call history while pairing your Oura Ring. This information
isn't needed, so you can toggle it off and tap Pair to continue
connecting your ring.

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You'll have to
spend at least one night sleeping with your Oura Ring on to receive
[Sleep](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025445574) and
[Readiness](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025589793)
Scores the following morning. However,
[Activity](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025577993)
data and the [Explore
content](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410672624019)
will all be available immediately.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Oura will
establish baseline readings over the next few weeks of your ring use,
which will inform the app's personalized guidance and insight messages.
Features such as
<a href="https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410663885331"
target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cycle Insights</a> and
[Restorative
Time](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410641295763) also
require ~30-60 days of consecutive data for optimal performance and
accurate readings.</span>

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Set Up a New Ring for Existing Members**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Follow these
steps if you've purchased a different model ring, upgraded from an Oura
Ring Gen3 to an Oura Ring 4, or
are</span><span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">
replacing your current
ring.</span><span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black"></span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">On mobile:
<a href="oura://v1/route/my_ring?page=details" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">click here to begin your ring setup</a>  
On desktop: follow the instructions below  
</span>

### <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Reset your old ring</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Note:** If
your old ring is lost or otherwise nonfunctional, you can skip these
reset instructions and jump to
**<a href="#h_01G417C1DBGBGJD1HQR8TM95RZ" target="_self">Set up a
replacement ring</a>.**</span>

1.  Ensure that you have a stable internet connection
2.  Place your ring on the charger for a few minutes
3.  Open the Oura App and wait for the latest sync
4.  Back up your data:
    - Tap on the <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized" style="width: 20px;"
      alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner of the home
      screen
    - Select Settings \> Back up all data
5.  When the backup is complete, take your ring off the charger and
    place it on a flat surface
6.  Factory reset your ring
    - Tap the <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="aspect-ratio: 48/35; width: 20px;" width="48" height="35"
      alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner \> My Oura
      Ring, or tap the
       <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872619027"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="aspect-ratio: 48/48; width: 18px;" width="48" height="48"
      alt="ring battery level icon" /> battery icon in the upper-right
      corner to go directly to the My Oura Ring page
    - Tap Factory reset
7.  When the factory reset is complete, leave the ring off the charger
    and keep it on a flat surface without moving it for two minutes.
    This allows the ring to enter power saving mode if you're storing
    the ring
8.  In your device's Bluetooth settings, remove your Oura Ring from the
    list of connected devices

### <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Set up a replacement ring</span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Place
    your new ring on the charger  **
    </span><span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If
    you skipped the above factory reset steps:</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Tap the
      <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
      class="guide-markup wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="box-sizing: inherit; font-weight: inherit; max-width: 100%; height: auto; aspect-ratio: 48 / 35; width: 20px;"
      width="48" height="35" alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the
      upper-left corner \> My Oura Ring, or tap the
      <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872619027"
      class="guide-markup wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="box-sizing: inherit; font-weight: inherit; max-width: 100%; height: auto; aspect-ratio: 48 / 48; width: 18px;"
      width="48" height="48" alt="ring battery level icon" /> battery
      icon in the upper-right to go directly to the My Oura Ring
      page</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Tap Set
      up a new ring</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Select
      Begin setup \> Skip \> Set up a new ring</span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">**Select
    Connect** and follow the in-app instructions to pair your new ring
    to Bluetooth and sync with your account</span>

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Transfer Your Ring and Data to a New Device**</span>

To transfer your ring data and Oura account to a new phone, tablet, or
other supported device:

### <span class="wysiwyg-font-color-black">Remove your ring from the old device</span>

1.  Ensure that you have a stable internet connection
2.  Place your ring on the charger until it reaches at least 50%
3.  Go to the <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
    class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized" style="width: 20px;"
    alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner of the Oura
    App's home screen
4.  Select **Settings**
5.  Scroll all the way down and select **Sign out**. Your app data will
    automatically be backed up during the sign out process
6.  When the signout is complete, go to your device's Bluetooth settings
    and remove your Oura Ring from the list of connected devices

### <span class="wysiwyg-font-color-black">Connect your ring to the new device</span>

7.  **Leave your ring on the charger**, connected to a power source
8.  **Install the Oura App** on your new phone
9.  **Follow the**
    <a href="/hc/en-us/articles/360058634153" target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>in-app instructions</strong></a>
    to log in to your account and connect your ring
10. Once you have successfully signed in with your current account email
    address, **uninstall the Oura App from your old phone**

If your old device is lost or otherwise inoperable, your ring will go
into Restricted Mode when attempting to pair with a new device. In this
case, the Oura App will prompt you to
<a href="/hc/en-us/articles/360025446854" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">factory reset the ring</a>, which will turn
off Restricted Mode. Data saved on the ring since your last sync will be
erased, but any historical data saved before that will still be
available.

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Transfer Your Ring to a New User**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you're
going to gift your used ring to someone else:</span>

1.  Ensure that you have a stable internet connection
2.  Place your ring on the charger for a few minutes
3.  Open the Oura App and wait for the latest sync
4.  Back up your data:
    - Tap on the <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized" style="width: 20px;"
      alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner of the home
      screen
    - Select Settings \> Back up all data
5.  When the backup is complete, take your ring off the charger and
    place it on a flat surface
6.  Factory reset your ring
    - Tap the <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="aspect-ratio: 48/35; width: 20px;" width="48" height="35"
      alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner \> My Oura
      Ring, or tap the
       <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872619027"
      class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="aspect-ratio: 48/48; width: 18px;" width="48" height="48"
      alt="ring battery level icon" /> battery icon in the upper-right
      corner to go directly to the My Oura Ring page
    - Tap Factory reset
7.  When the factory reset is complete, leave the ring off the charger
    and keep it on a flat surface without moving it for two minutes.
    This allows the ring to enter power saving mode if you're storing
    the ring
8.  In your device's Bluetooth settings, remove your Oura Ring from the
    list of connected devices

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">The ring is
now ready to be paired to a new device. Note that Oura Membership is not
transferable between accounts, so the new user will need to create their
own account when setting up their ring. </span>

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Troubleshooting**</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-color-black"><span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If
your ring is not connecting to your device, make sure your charger is
plugged in correctly and your device's Bluetooth is
enabled.</span></span>

<span class="wysiwyg-color-black"><span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If
none of these steps resolves your issue, you can:</span></span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Uninstall
    and reinstall the Oura App</span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Restart
    your device</span>
3.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">[Factory
    reset your
    ring](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025446854)  
    </span>

### <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Trouble connecting</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you
experience difficulty connecting your new ring, check out our
<a href="https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025592013"
target="_blank" rel="noopener">Troubleshooting Connection Issues
article</a>.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you
encounter any difficulties while updating your firmware, check out
<a href="https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/sections/9686727101843"
target="_blank" rel="noopener">the firmware updates articles</a> for
tips on how to successfully complete your update. If you're still
experiencing problems, please reach out to our team.</span>

**You can
<a href="#" onclick="window.duet.open();">contact our Member Care Team
here</a>.**

The intended use and setup of an Oura Ring should consist of the
following: one ring, one account, one device. If there are multiple
users or data sources feeding into the same account, this leads to
missing data, connectivity issues, or even accelerated battery
consumption.

### <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Identifying your ring model</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">In case you've
purchased the same model and finish as your prior ring and are looking
to identify which ring is which:</span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Place one
    of your rings on its charger</span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Go to the
    My Oura Ring page by tapping on the
    <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872627603"
    class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
    style="aspect-ratio: 48/35; width: 20px;" width="48" height="35"
    alt="Icon Bars Menu" /> menu in the upper-left corner or the battery
    <img src="/hc/article_attachments/36087872619027"
    class="image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
    style="aspect-ratio: 48/48; width: 18px;" width="48" height="48"
    alt="ring battery level icon" /> icon in the upper-right corner of
    the home screen </span>
3.  <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">Swipe left to view your model and
    size, ring generation, and serial number</span>
