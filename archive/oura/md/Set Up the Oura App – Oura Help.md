# Set Up the Oura App

<div class="article-author">

<div class="article-meta">

</div>

</div>

</div>

<div class="article-content">

<div class="article-body">

The Oura App is supported on iOS devices running Apple iOS 15 and
higher, or Android devices running Android 9 and higher with Google Play
services. Your device must support Bluetooth 4.0.

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Set Up Your Oura Account **</span>

<figure
class="image wysiwyg-image image-style-block-align-left wysiwyg-image-block-start image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
style="width: 4.32%;">
<img src="/hc/article_attachments/33074327834003"
class="image-style-align-left wysiwyg-image-align-start wysiwyg-image-inline image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
style="aspect-ratio: 116/140;" width="116" height="140"
alt="Oura logo.svg" />
</figure>

1.  Download the free Oura App from the [App
    Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oura/id1043837948) (iOS) or the
    [Google Play
    Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ouraring.oura&hl=en_US)
    (Android)
2.  Open the Oura App and tap **Start**

<figure class="image wysiwyg-image image_resized wysiwyg-image-resized"
style="width: 300px;">
<img src="/hc/article_attachments/22172758781459" width="300"
alt="a screenshot of the Oura App&#39;s start screen/splash page" />
</figure>

3.  If you're new to Oura, enter an email address to use for your Oura
    account and tap **Continue**
    - This email can differ from the one used to purchase your Oura Ring
      and will serve as your login across all Oura services, including
      your membership
    - If you're already an Oura member, enter the email associated with
      your Oura account and follow the login prompts
    - If you purchased a prepaid membership with your ring, it will
      automatically be applied to the first Oura account you create
      during setup. Once the membership is linked to an account, it
      cannot be transferred to a different Oura account
4.  On the next screen, accept the **terms and conditions** by selecting
    the first toggle. If you'd like to receive Oura usage tips and
    special offers in your email, select the second toggle as well
5.  To set up a new Oura Ring, follow the in-app instructions or see
    <a href="/hc/en-us/articles/4411128662291" target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer">this article</a> for more information
6.  Once you've completed setting up your Oura Ring, tap **Send setup
    email**
7.  Check your inbox for an account set up email. In the email, select
    the **Set up account button** or **the verification link** to take
    you to a verification page
8.  Enter the **billing address** associated with a payment method
    you'll use for your [Oura
    Membership](/hc/en-us/articles/4409086524819)
    - Some members are not required to enter billing or payment
      information (for example, members with Lifetime Membership)
9.  Enter your **home address** (required for sales tax calculations)
10. Select your **billing preference** (monthly or annual) and then
    **enter a payment method**
    - You won't be charged until the end of your first complimentary
      month. If you have a prepaid membership, you won't be charged
      until the end of your prepaid period
11. Review and confirm that the information you've entered is correct,
    then tap **Submit**
12. Return to the Oura App and tap **Continue**. If you're using an
    iPhone, you'll have the option to enable facial or touch recognition
    for faster sign-in

Once you've returned to the Oura App, your account and membership setup
are complete.

<span style="color: #000000;">Continue on to the next screen in the Oura
App</span> to [personalize your Oura
experience](#01HD2C34BTFKSV1SP6646NQ3AT)
<span style="color: #000000;">and finish the app setup.</span>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## <span class="wysiwyg-color-black">**Personalize Your Oura Experience**</span>

1.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">On the
    next screen, select **Begin** to sync your data. This process may
    take a few minutes</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If the
      Oura App asks you to set up a new Oura Ring, follow the in-app
      instructions or see
      <a href="/hc/en-us/articles/4411128662291" target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer">this article</a> for more
      information</span>
2.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">You'll
    have the option to link your Oura App to third-party integrations,
    including Apple Health and Health Connect by Android. For more
    information, see our articles on
    <a href="https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/sections/9721508785171"
    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">using app integrations</a>
    with Oura</span>
3.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Enter your
    **Date of birth**, **Weight**, **Height**, and **Sex assigned at
    birth**. Tap on your desired measurement system (imperial or metric)
    under **Units**, then tap **Done**</span>
    - <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you
      decide to use a third-party app like Apple Health or Health
      Connect by Android, this personal information will update
      automatically from there. You will not be able to update this
      within the Oura App in the future</span>
4.  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">If you are
    new to Oura, take a moment to answer some questions so that Oura can
    personalize your experience</span>

<a href="/hc/article_attachments/22172758785043" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer"><img
src="/hc/article_attachments/22172758785043" width="300" height="649"
alt="a screenshot of the Oura App showing a list of options from which to choose your focus area" /></a>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">Your Oura App
is now set up.</span>

<span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large wysiwyg-color-black">For the best
experience with Oura, set up your country of residence under Settings.
Due to regulation, some features are country-specific. You can customize
or change units, notifications, data sharing, and other preferences
anytime in the Settings menu.</span>

the different features with
<a href="/hc/en-us/articles/360058599753" target="_blank"
rel="noopener noreferrer">How to Use the Oura App</a>.</span>

rel="noopener noreferrer">troubleshooting</a> steps.</span>
