continuous high-resolution raw acceleration data, sampled up to 256 Hz,
to support the development of novel endpoints related to real-world
activity, mobility, and sleep behaviors to provide objective, real-world
physical activity, mobility, and sleep measures, in near real time.  
Used in conjunction with the CentrePoint Data Hub or mobile application,
captured data is passively transmitted to the cloud for near real-time
detection of compliance issues and potential data trends.
