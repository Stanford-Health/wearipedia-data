# What are the recommended G6 iPhone settings?

<div class="css-9httdy">

<div class="slate-content">

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Your phone settings can prevent
the Dexcom G6 app from working. Learn more about the settings that will
allow your app to work as expected.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Screen Time</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">On iOS 12 and above, Screen Time's
Downtime and App Limits can temporarily disable apps. If you set time
limits for the Dexcom G6 app, you may not open the app, get alerts or
sensor readings. Either:</span>

</div>

- 
- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Turn off Downtime and App Limits
  or</span>
- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Add Dexcom G6 app to the Always
  Allowed app list</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to add G6 to allowed
apps</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, find Screen Time,
choose Always Allowed, find Dexcom G6, and tap the plus icon to add
Dexcom G6 to the Allowed Apps list.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Do Not Disturb</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Do Not Disturb silences all alerts
except for the Urgent Low alarm, Transmitter Failure, Sensor Failure,
and App Stopped Alert. If the Dexcom G6 Always Sound feature is on, it
will override your phone settings, so you hear glucose alerts set to
sound.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Do Not Disturb
off</span>

</div>

- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, tap on Do Not
  Disturb, and turn it off.</span>
- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">To turn Always Sound on, open
  your Dexcom G6 app, tap Settings, choose Alerts, and turn Always Sound
  on.</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Low Power Mode</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Low Power Mode may prevent the
Dexcom G6 app from running in the background. When Low Power Mode is on,
your Dexcom G6 alarm and alerts may be delayed. If you're using Share,
information sent to followers may also be delayed. We do not recommend
using Low Power Mode.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Low Power Mode
off</span>

</div>

- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, tap Battery, and
  turn Low Power Mode off.</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Device Bluetooth</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Your Dexcom G6 app uses Bluetooth
to connect with your transmitter. You must keep your phone Bluetooth
turned on to get alerts and sensor readings.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Bluetooth on</span>

</div>

- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, find Bluetooth,
  and turn it on.</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">App Bluetooth Permission</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">On iOS 13 and above, Apple
requires you to give the Dexcom G6 app permission to use Bluetooth. You
must keep Bluetooth Permission turned on for the Dexcom G6 app to
work.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Bluetooth Permission
on</span>

</div>

- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, tap on Dexcom
  G6, and turn Bluetooth on.</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Notifications Permission</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Notifications let you get alarm
and alerts on your phone. If notifications for the Dexcom G6 app are
off, you won't get any alarm or alerts. We recommend turning Dexcom G6
app notifications on.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Notifications
on</span>

</div>

- <span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, choose Dexcom
  G6, tap Notifications, turn Allow Notifications on.</span>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">Background App Refresh</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Background App Refresh allows the
Dexcom G6 app to continue running in the background. If Background App
Refresh is turned off, your Dexcom G6 alarm and alerts may be delayed.
If you're using Share, information sent to followers may also be
delayed. We recommend keeping Background App Refresh turned on.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-14la0iu">How to turn Background App Refresh
on</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">Go to Settings, find Dexcom G6,
and turn Background App Refresh on.</span>

</div>

<div class="paragraph css-12595yo">

<span class="chakra-text css-1btamed">The location of each setting may
be different based on your iOS version. Please refer to your smart
device instructions for detailed information. Not sure which iOS version
you're on? Find your device's iOS version within the About section of
your phone's Settings.</span>

</div>
